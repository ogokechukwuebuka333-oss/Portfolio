How I work with AI

I use AI to explore faster. I use design judgment to decide what ships.

AI helps me cover more ground early: more flows, more scenarios, more questions. It doesn't decide anything. Every output gets challenged before it becomes a design.

  1. Understand the problem

    Clarify requirements, users, business constraints and the questions nobody has answered yet.

  2. Explore the system

    Use AI to explore possible flows and surface scenarios worth investigating.

  3. Challenge the output

    Check assumptions, consistency, accessibility, edge cases and how failure is handled.

  4. Design and validate

    Refine the experience, prototype the interactions and test the decisions that matter, where possible.

  5. Prepare for implementation

    Document behaviours, states, dependencies, open questions and what engineering needs.

A real example · BetAlmighty
Caught a gap

The AI-drafted flow for 1v1 bets had no way to back out once the stakes were locked. I spotted it in review and designed mutual cancellation: nothing is refunded until both players agree.

Rejected a suggestion

The AI backed a review note arguing the betting hub looked like a bookmaker, and proposed reworking it. I judged the analysis wrong and told it to drop the change.

About

Close to the people who use it

I start with how a product behaves: who can do what, what state things are in, and what happens when something goes wrong. The screens come after that, and they're easier to get right.

I've designed across edtech, fintech, proptech, music, e-commerce and healthcare, and I'm most at home where the rules are complicated: money in escrow, five kinds of user sharing one system, or AI doing part of the work. Alongside design, I work directly with users. At Soundyaad I help independent artists actually adopt the product, which keeps me honest about what people need.

I studied Accounting & Finance before moving into design, and I co-founded Cruize Central, Soundyaad's parent company, where I still lead the graphic design.
