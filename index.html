BetAlmighty®

A Nigerian betting app where friends stake against each other and the money waits in escrow until both sides agree on the result.

RoleProduct design
PlatformiOS app
Scope135 screens · 5 product areas
MarketNigeria · NGN + crypto
Overview

One app, four ways to play

BetAlmighty combines a lottery, peer-to-peer betting, a betting community and a naira and crypto wallet. The bottom navigation gives each its own tab: Home, Lottery, Betting, Community and Wallet.

Challenge 01

Holding friends' money

When two people bet against each other, someone has to hold the stakes and someone has to decide who won. Both had to feel fair.

Challenge 02

Nigerian payment realities

Bank transfer is the default, card charges get declined, and many players hold USDT. Funding and withdrawals had to work on all three.

Challenge 03

Gambling harm

Making betting social also makes it easier to overdo. Limits and breaks had to sit inside the core flows, not in a settings page.

Principles that shaped every screen

Show where the money is

Every bet screen states what's in escrow. A Committed Funds view lists every locked naira and why.

Every failure has a next step

A declined card offers bank transfer. A BVN mismatch lists exactly what to check.

Your money stays yours

Breaks, loss limits and disputes pause betting, but withdrawals stay open.

Warn before it's irreversible

Crypto withdrawals carry a wrong-network warning and a 24-hour hold on new addresses.

Onboarding & identity

Start light, earn trust, then unlock more

Four intro slides set expectations: bet with mates, escrow-held funds, NGN and crypto, big jackpots. Sign-up is four steps: details matched to the name on your BVN, password, phone code, then BVN. BVN is optional at sign-up. Skipping it lands players on Tier 1 (₦50,000 daily deposits, withdrawals locked) so they can try the product before sharing identity data.

Verification is tiered (BVN, then ID, then address), and each tier raises limits. The KYC screen shows the current tier, what the next one needs and how long review takes.

Escrow

I designed the state machine before the screens

Money in a personal bet can be in ten states. I mapped them for engineering first, so every screen, notification and button maps to exactly one state and nothing falls between two.

Amber: funds held Green: released Red: conflict Grey: paused
  • Both confirmations match: the pot is released instantly, with no admin involved.
  • They don't match: the bet becomes a dispute. An admin reviews the evidence both sides upload, usually within 24 hours.
  • Evidence is unclear: funds stay on hold until clearer evidence arrives or both agree to cancel.
  • Either player can propose cancelling. If both agree, each stake goes back in full with no fee.

The same colours carry into the app, so players can read a bet's state at a glance.

Group bets

Pots that explain themselves

In a group bet the backers of the winning outcome split the pot, so payouts shift as people join. The join screen shows what you'd win on each outcome, live, with the reminder "fewer backers = bigger share". The number locks when the bet closes. If too few players join before the deadline, the bet cancels itself and every stake is refunded in full.

Wallet

Money in and out on three rails

Funding: bank transfer to a dedicated account number, a debit card stored as a token by Paystack (₦50 verification charge, refunded) with the bank's one-time code, or USDT on TRC20, ERC20 or BEP20 via a QR address. Withdrawals: a review screen with fee and arrival date, a 6-digit SMS code, then a status tracker from request to credit.

Play responsibly

Safety built into the flow, not buried in settings

The Play Responsibly hub shows this week's time in the app, deposits and net result, and holds every tool: weekly deposit and loss limits, session reminders, breaks from 24 hours to 30 days, self-exclusion from 6 months to permanent, and a free, confidential support line.

The hub

One place for every limit

Progress bars show how much of each weekly limit is used, so a player sees where they stand before a limit stops them.

Session reminder

A pause with the numbers

After an hour, a sheet shows stakes, wins and net result, with Take a Break as the main action and Keep Playing beneath it.

Deposit limit

Options instead of a dead end

Deposit what's left of the cap, wait for the weekly reset, or raise the limit. A raise takes effect after 24 hours, never instantly.

Break

A break that means it

Betting and deposits are blocked, while withdrawals stay open and open bets still settle. The break can't be ended early.

Loss limit

Stops at the line you drew

New bets and tickets pause until the period resets, and the limit can't be raised mid-period. Withdrawals are never blocked.

"A break cannot be ended early. Not by you, not by support. That is what makes it worth setting."Copy from the Break Active screen

Lottery

A lottery that pays out by itself

Weekly and daily draws with a visible jackpot and tickets-sold progress. Players pick 5 numbers from 1 to 36 or use Quick Pick, pay ₦500 from their wallet, and see prize tiers for 3, 4 and 5 matches. Winnings are credited automatically. There's nothing to claim.

Community

Social, with guardrails

Topic communities (sports, entertainment, politics, business) each post their rules, starting with no selling betslips and no fixed-match claims. Posts can attach a prediction others can agree with, and reporting has a dedicated "scam or fixed-match claims" reason. Direct messages are limited to people who share a community, and strangers land in Message Requests. A 1v1 challenge can be sent straight from a chat.

Design system

Dark-first tokens with gold as the brand

A near-black canvas, #1A1A1A cards and a gold primary. Bricolage Grotesque Bold carries the display moments (logo, wallet balances, page titles), and Geist runs the interface, small and dense. Cards, buttons and inputs share a 20px radius, on an iPhone 17 frame of 402 × 874.
