How I work

From a requirements doc to a handoff

This is how BetAlmighty came together. I used AI to draft the flows quickly, then reviewed them, redesigned the screens in Figma and wrote up what engineering needed.

  1. Requirements in

    User stories, constraints and the rules nobody wrote down yet.

  2. AI-drafted flows

    Mid-fidelity flow boards for every story, each state side by side.

  3. Critique and decide

    AI as a second reviewer. I keep what holds up and set aside what doesn't.

  4. Design in Figma

    Final screens, the visual language and the token system.

  5. Handoff notes

    Every assumed rule, open question and risk, written down before build.

See it in the BetAlmighty case study
  1. Map behaviour before pixels

    I chart every state a product can be in, then draw the screens, so nothing falls between two states.

  2. Every failure has a next step

    A declined card offers another way to pay. A failed check says exactly what to fix. Dead ends cost trust.

  3. Always show where things stand

    What's locked, what's pending and what happens next should never be a mystery to the person waiting.
